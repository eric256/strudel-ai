Describe the music you want and the AI will rewrite the code live.
Try: “dark techno at 128 bpm”, “add a jazzy chord progression”, “make the hats swing”, “drop everything but the bass”.
Changes switch in on the bar line (see “switch on” at the top). Click anywhere once to enable audio. Ctrl+Enter in the editor re-evaluates your own edits.

Songs — one song per line: title | description (style, tempo, key, mood…). For each song the AI writes a song sheet (chords, hook, parts, form) and the parts once; the app then arranges the sections from them, so choruses repeat and the song stays in key. The next song is written while the current one plays. Click a song to see its sheet and sections.

The instructions (system prompts) the AI gets for each job. Edit them to change its style or rules; your versions are saved in this browser and used instead of the built-in ones. The list of loaded sounds is still added automatically.

A form is the order and length of a song's sections. The AI picks the form that fits each song's genre (or the one you choose in the Songs / Station tab) and copies its sections and bar counts exactly. Saved in this browser.

A station is a name and a theme; the 📻 Station tab plays it.

Download everything this browser has saved (settings, song forms, stations, pads, layout, last code) as a file, or load such a file — e.g. on another device or another address of this server.
