Song blocks — the sections of one song, one per line: bars | instruction. Code for every block is generated ahead of time (each builds on the previous one) and switched in exactly on the bar line. Click ⏭ go (or press Alt+1…9) to jump to any block.

Set list — one song per line: title | description (style, tempo, key, mood…). While a song plays, the AI writes the next song's blocks and code, and switches to it on the bar line when the current song ends.

Station — give it a theme and it runs on its own: an AI agent keeps inventing new songs for the theme, writes their blocks and code ahead of time, and plays them one after another.

Describe the music you want and the AI will rewrite the code live.
Try: “dark techno at 128 bpm”, “add a jazzy chord progression”, “make the hats swing”, “drop everything but the bass”.
Changes switch in on the bar line (see “switch on” at the top). Click anywhere once to enable audio. Ctrl+Enter in the editor re-evaluates your own edits.
